# Establish asset-use authorization

Type: wayfinder:task
Status: open

## Question

Can the user establish a directly applicable authorization for a local,
non-distributed independent importer of the selected build-12340 World of
Warcraft assets, including any transient or cached transformations? If not,
should this effort stop at format research or redraw its destination around
project-owned assets?

## Required human actions

1. Retain private evidence of lawful acquisition and applicable user control;
   do not add it to the repository.
2. Obtain written Blizzard authorization covering the exact importer/cache use,
   or obtain qualified legal advice for the user's jurisdiction and activity.
3. Record only the resulting yes/no decision and permitted boundaries here.

The agent must not contact Blizzard, counsel, or another third party without
separate user authorization.
