# Research the private-use and provenance boundary

Type: wayfinder:research
Status: resolved

## Question

What official Blizzard terms, applicable copyright constraints, and project
controls define the safe private-learning boundary for reading a legally
obtained local build-12340 asset installation and retaining a regenerable local
cache, without distributing raw or derived content?

## Answer

The full assessment is recorded in
[Private-use and provenance boundary](../research/01-private-use-and-provenance-boundary.md).

Published terms do not give this project an affirmative basis to treat private
local importing/caching as authorized. Neither local candidate can establish
legal provenance from its contents. Private local controls remain required but
are risk reduction, not permission; **Establish asset-use authorization** is
now the human gate before any importer, cache, or rendered Blizzard content is
implemented or run.
